Arkham Deck Helper

for Arkham Horror: The Card Game: build a deck from your collection, or check one before the Mythos finds its weak spots.

Build a deck Check a deck Chaos bag

Paste a text export from ArkhamDB or arkham.build, ArkhamDB's JSON, or a plain list like 2x Machete. Put the investigator's name on its own line. Add (2) after a card to mean its level 2 version.

Works for published decklists and decks set to public. Private decks: use “Copy as text” on ArkhamDB and paste it instead.

Card data from arkhamdb-json-data, card images from ArkhamDB. Arkham Horror: The Card Game is © Fantasy Flight Games. This is an unofficial fan tool. The grades are rules of thumb, not gospel.
